# Vrinda-Store-Annual-Sales-Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the annual sales performance of Vrinda Store (2022) using Microsoft Excel.
The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights by performing data cleaning, processing, analysis, and dashboard creation.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to answer key business questions and support data-driven decision-making.

🛠 Tools & Technologies

Microsoft Excel

Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts

Slicers (Month, Category, Channel)

Data Cleaning & Data Processing

🔄 Data Preparation & Processing

Removed duplicate records and handled missing values

Standardized fields such as gender, age group, order status, and sales channels

Created new calculated columns for:

Age groups

Month

Built Pivot Tables to support dashboard visuals and insights

❓ Business Questions Answered

The dashboard was designed to answer the following key questions:

Compare Sales and Orders using a single chart.

Which month recorded the highest sales and orders?

Who purchased more in 2022 — Men or Women?

What are the different order statuses in 2022?

List the top 5 states contributing to total sales.

What is the relationship between age group and gender based on number of orders?

Which sales channel contributes the maximum sales?

Which is the highest selling product category?

📊 Dashboard Features

Monthly comparison of Orders vs Sales

Sales distribution by Gender

Order status analysis (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, Refunded)

Top States contributing to revenue

Orders split by Age Group and Gender

Channel-wise sales performance

Interactive slicers for Month, Category, and Channel

🔍 Key Insights

Women customers contribute nearly 65% of total sales

Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 revenue-generating states

The Adult age group (30–49 years) contributes around 50% of total orders

Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the highest-performing sales channels

Most orders are successfully delivered, indicating strong operational efficiency

📈 Final Business Recommendation

To improve Vrinda Store sales:

Focus marketing efforts on women aged 30–49 years living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh by offering targeted ads, discounts, and coupons on high-performing platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
